On “When files are hand-edited after the initial import, which is the source of truth for show and set: (A) the files on disk, (B) the stored projection, or (C) something else?”: A. The files are the source of truth. `show` and `set` always read the files as they are on disk. The stored projection is only the change history, so a hand edit appears in `show` but produces no history event. A later `set` records its `old` value as whatever was in the file.
